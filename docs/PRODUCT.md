# Product

## Platform

ios

## Users

Creators discovering and composing live visual camera effects, then capturing the result.

## Product Purpose

grey//VISION is a live visual-effects camera. Its core workflow is effect discovery, composition, and capture.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The iPhone workspace contains a live camera, effect selection, parameter editing, stacking, and capture controls.
- Preserve the existing rendering, stacking, saved effect identities, and capture behavior during UI refinement.
- The Mac remote panel is outside the current phone effects-menu refinement.
- Camera, LiDAR, and Objects are capture sources; effects and slots form a Composition. See [domain language](CONTEXT.md).

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the current workspace layout.
- The effects menu should feel quiet, soft, polished, and premium, with actual previews and consolidated navigation rather than overwhelming simultaneous information.

## Product Principles

- Show an effect before asking the creator to choose it.
- Reveal detailed information when needed rather than presenting everything at once.
- Keep capture and composition behavior dependable while refining discovery.

## Open Decisions

No product-specific accessibility standard or additional audience has been confirmed. Existing VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support must remain intact.
