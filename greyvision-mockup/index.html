What grey//VISION is

grey//VISION is a live visual-effects camera. Open the app, point your iPhone, and watch a chosen effect live on screen — a filter you can layer, tune, and capture.

The interface is designed as a quiet camera-grade instrument: effects stack, you can search them by name or description, and every setting you set stays in a Composition you control.

How advanced is it — in plain terms

Real live effects, not static previews

Every effect runs through Metal on your iPhone, so the picture on screen is what you see — live parameter dials, and stacked effects combine. There is no render queue or delay between you and the camera.

Composition: camera, depth, effects

Sources come from your iPhone camera — and where the device offers them, depth (LiDAR). Effects and slots form a Composition you control: drag to reorder, duplicate or remove. The Mac companion's story is the same idea, one step further.

Search and recents, for 133 effects

The search box matches effect names and descriptions. RECENTS remembers your last ten effects, one tap away.

A waypoint to Mac — the local link

The Mac companion receives the processed iPhone feed over your home Wi-Fi: a 6-digit pairing code, then a live HEVC preview at about 24 fps. Nothing leaves your machine.

Tunable Mac quality

While connected, flip between BAL (12 Mbps) and STUDIO (20 Mbps). A keyframe refresh recovers a late join without a full reconnect.

A future path that's on hold

A system-wide virtual camera exists in the repo (Camera Extension) but is not active — Apple requires a paid developer team before it can be used by Photo Booth, FaceTime, or Blackmagic.
